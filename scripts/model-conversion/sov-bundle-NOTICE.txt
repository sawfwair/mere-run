model and OpenAI's Whisper large-v3 audio encoder. Their publisher model cards

https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3

Modifications by Sawfwair, 2026: MoE expert matrices were converted to affine
Q8 group-64 packed weights with fused gate/up projections for mere.run.
Audio encoder/projector tensors were extracted into a separate safetensors file
without changing their tensor bytes. Target parameter names were mapped to the
native Swift/MLX runtime. The optional DFlash checkpoint is unchanged.
Upstream revisions and conversion details are recorded in conversion.json.
