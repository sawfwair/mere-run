# LFM2.5 24B-A2B: 18–19 GB quality experiment

This experiment targets a smaller native MLX bundle for a 36 GB Mac while
preserving the [23.94 GB mixed 6/8-bit control](./lfm25-24b-a2b-integration-2026-10-13.md).
The selected bundle contains 18,926,985,216 logical weight bytes and
18,945,198,294 total bytes (18.95 decimal GB). It is about 21% smaller than
the control. Construction, remote evaluation, and bounded local verification are
complete. The strict source-quality and native/CUDA parity gates do not pass;
this artifact remains experimental.

## Immutable inputs and control

The source is `LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B` at
`1769516ac588c4c558089c94f27d96b0dc67b183`. All ten complete source shards and
original metadata passed the original size and SHA-256 checks.

The existing control remains unchanged. A separate RunPod reproduction matches
all 41 shards, config, and conversion receipt byte for byte. Its conversion
receipt SHA-256 is
`3c277d55ee8b50a5c08a4d6f80f9320e651c5d44f390bccf7c3558bd711654be`.
The reproduced control therefore has identical quantized values and pinned
source provenance.

The final diagnostic corpus has 260 calibration cases and 152 heldout cases,
with 14,062 and 7,733 scored tokens respectively. It combines the original
60-case multilingual/chat diagnostic, deterministic authored record and code
cases, and prose-continuation windows from WikiText train and test splits.
WikiText is pinned to `Salesforce/wikitext` revision
`b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3`. Its notices and generated passages
remain in experiment storage. This is a diagnostic corpus, not a broad
capability benchmark.

The frozen token suite SHA-256 is
`31979a01082d2ae71ebb04e444c2d2746a5349e0fff606167cebebac14c0ce34`.
Heldout inputs do not contribute to activation statistics, allocation, or
candidate selection. Source and candidate scoring use identical tokens,
FP32 activation arithmetic, disabled TF32, and all 128,000 vocabulary logits.
The source uses its original BF16 weight values promoted to FP32; each candidate
uses its stored quantized values.

The CUDA reference expands stored affine expert codes once into FP32 and uses
dense expert GEMMs. On 48 control cases with 1,331 scored tokens, this produces
bit-identical full-vocabulary logits to the fused CUDA quantized kernels. This
reference optimization does not change the artifact or the native packed
Metal execution path. That bit-exact proof covers those 48 short control cases;
it does not establish equivalence for every longer case or the selected
5/6-bit artifact.

## Calibration and allocation

Routed activation statistics cover 118,977 input tokens. Every expert in all
38 sparse layers was visited; the minimum routed count is 37. Covariances
weight inputs by the square of their unbiased normalized routing score.

The initial search evaluates affine 4-, 5-, and 6-bit packing at group sizes
32 and 64, plus 8-bit group 64. It selects clipping from a fixed grid,
applies group-local Hessian error feedback, and fits affine coefficients
against the measured activation covariance. Scales and biases remain FP16.
Original embeddings, dense prefix layers, attention, convolutions, routers,
and norms retain their BF16 values, with FP32 router correction biases.
The bundle explicitly requests FP32 activations.

Five allocation families target at most 18.95 billion logical weight bytes:
all options, group 32, group 64, 4/6-bit group 32, and 5/6-bit group 64.
Reconstruction error only proposes allocations. Full-vocabulary calibration
KL selects the best tested model within the required 18–19 billion byte range.
The 23.94 GB control is scored but cannot win the smaller size budget.

A second pass refines the selected allocation using full-column activation
covariances, descending activation order, one percent diagonal damping, and
cross-block error feedback. It keeps the selected bits, groups, FP16 affine
coefficients, and logical size. Each output row retains its original codes
unless the new codes lower its full-covariance calibration error. Calibration
KL determines whether to retain the refinement. This does not guarantee that
heldout KL improves.

The final policy and its hashes are frozen before source, control, and selected
heldout evaluation. Existing absolute quality gates remain unchanged:
mean KL at most 0.01, per-case mean KL at most 0.03, top-token agreement at
least 98%, aggregate target perplexity ratio at most 1.01, and per-case ratio
at most 1.05. Both models fail these absolute gates and retain `quality_qualified: false`.

## Remote results

Selection uses calibration only. The full-column refinement lowers calibration
KL from 0.041179 to 0.025886, a 37% improvement over the initial best allocation.
It was frozen before heldout evaluation. This is the best of six tested smaller
candidates, not a claim of a global optimum.

| Model | Calibration KL | Heldout KL | Heldout top-token agreement | Heldout target perplexity ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 23.94 GB control | 0.025408 | 0.023487 | 93.82% | 0.992043 |
| 18.95 GB selected | 0.025886 | 0.034681 | 92.65% | 0.995612 |

All comparisons use the same source reference. The smaller artifact loses about
1.16 percentage points of heldout top-token agreement and has higher distribution
KL. Its target perplexity is about 0.36% worse relative to the control on this
corpus. Perplexity below the source does not establish better general capability.
Both artifacts exceed the absolute KL/top-token and worst-case gates.
The authored records/code heldout subset improves (KL 0.016544 versus 0.023476),
while prose worsens (KL 0.042756 versus 0.026669). The original chat diagnostic
also worsens (KL 0.026057 versus 0.011957). These are diagnostic differences,
not task-accuracy scores.

The selected artifact has 70 expert projection banks at 5 bits and 44 at 6 bits,
all group 64, with FP16 scales and biases. All 314 non-expert tensors retain
source values and dtypes. The bundle contains 656 tensors across 115 shards.
Every bundle file passed SHA-256 verification after transfer; the local copy
contains real files and is self-contained.

CUDA generation completed format, Python square-function, and tool-call probes
to EOS. The arithmetic wording exhausted 512 reasoning tokens, and the 8K probe
had only 128 output tokens available, ending before its final answer. These two

retests use neutral wording and a larger output budget; the original probes

because the original host had no free GPU; no diagnostic GPU run started.

## Verification boundaries

All 412 frozen cases pass native tokenizer and prompt parity. Four native
mixed-precision tests pass on the Mac's Metal GPU, including 4/5/6-bit gather
parity and the existing 6/8-bit paths. Packing and budget/split checks pass. Full-Hessian
feedback passes positive-definite covariance checks for 4/5/6/8-bit codes,
including preservation of parent codes and affine metadata.

A scoped FP32 A2B decode policy reclaims disposable MLX allocation buffers at
1 GiB, or at least 256 MiB when near the existing memory budget. It does not
change live weights, KV state, precision, admission, or OS GPU limits. Its unit
test and the full software gate pass.

The first local retest was refused at 15.41 GB of admission headroom, below the
unchanged 16 GiB floor. After the user closed applications, normal headroom
recovered to 16.96 GiB and generation ran on the native Metal runtime.

| Local probe | Final result | Generated tokens | Decode tokens/s | Additional peak host swap |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Calculate 17 + 25 | Correct: 42 | 109 | 7.20 | 0.87 GB |
| Python square function | Valid function returning x * x | 263 | 12.93 | 1.25 GB |
| Original arithmetic format stress | Exhausted reasoning budget; no final answer | 768 | 13.60 | 0.68 GB |
| 2K window, 1,270 prompt tokens | Code recovered; ASCII hyphen replaced by U+2011 | 205 | 14.89 | 0.26 GB |
| 8K window, 7,410 prompt tokens | Same exact-copy error | 339 | 14.29 | 0.66 GB |

The earlier pre-policy format-stress run also exhausted 768 reasoning tokens,
at 9.42 tokens/s, and grew swap by 8.02 GB. The later run shows substantially
less swap growth, but background applications also changed; this is not an
isolated causal benchmark of the policy. Swap figures are incremental peaks
over each run's baseline, not total machine swap or Metal allocations. These
small stochastic probes establish bounded behavior, not broad task accuracy.
Fresh CLI processes took about 28–32 seconds to their first generated token;
decode throughput does not include that startup/prefill cost.

The full repository gate passes 5,452 XCTest cases (451 skipped, no failures)
and 194 Swift Testing cases across seven suites, plus lint, build, CLI help,
package policy, and hygiene checks. These software checks do not establish
local full-model inference.

The native full-checkpoint test completed all **412 cases / 21,795 scored tokens**
and both opt-in tests with no failures or skips. It took 1,041 seconds including
the test launcher. MLX allocation peaked at 20,927,713,564 bytes (20.93 GB), and
process physical footprint at 22,379,592,464 bytes (22.38 GB). Host swap rose from
6.51 GB to 10.50 GB, a 3.98 GB incremental peak. This is bounded fit evidence for
this workload and background load, not a zero-swap capacity claim.

The full teacher-forced comparison against the selected CUDA reference has
mean KL **0.000051134**, worst-case mean KL **0.005435**, and top-token agreement
**99.9679%** (seven differing positions out of 21,795). The predefined backend
gates require mean KL at most 0.000001, case KL at most 0.00001, and top-token
agreement at least 99.99%; **the backend gate fails**. Twenty-one cases exceed
mean KL 0.000001. The early one-case match did not predict the full result.

Against the source, native heldout KL is 0.034658, top-token agreement is 92.71%,
and target perplexity ratio is 0.995703. Those values are close to the CUDA
artifact measurements but still fail the source-relative quality gates.
Neither the weight-file audit nor successful execution establishes strict
backend fidelity. The cause of the rare larger differences is not isolated;
expert partition/reduction order, batch shape, and backend arithmetic remain
possible explanations. No threshold was relaxed and the artifact policy was
not changed using heldout outputs.

The 2K and 8K probes completed reasoning and final answers. Both recovered the
code's content but substituted a nonbreaking hyphen for its literal ASCII
hyphen, so exact-copy checks fail. Additional explicit-ASCII probes and a
partition-order diagnostic were refused by normal memory/disk admission while
other workloads ran. They provide no additional inference result. The routing
source remains the exact version used by the completed 412-case run; the
unvalidated ordering hypothesis is preserved separately in experiment storage.

See the [curated native receipt](./receipts/lfm25-18gb-native-verification-2026-10-15.json).
Raw full-vocabulary logits, host samples, CLI output, binary hashes, and failed
admission attempts remain on T7. The shared 16 GiB memory / 8 GiB disk floor and
OS GPU limits are unchanged. Full 131,072-token context, broad task accuracy,
long-case packed/expanded CUDA equivalence, and strict backend fidelity remain
unqualified.

No managed model, public upload, or release is created by this experiment.
